2. What Route does and does not do

Route takes published computer forecasts of wind, waves and tidal streams, a performance model of your boat and the course you choose, and calculates routes and finish times. Every input is an estimate.

  • Forecast models can be wrong, out of date or missing, and can disagree strongly with each other and with what you meet at sea.
  • Tidal stream, depth and coastline data are approximate. Route does not add the height of tide to depths and does not know every hazard.
  • Traffic separation schemes, keep-out areas and marks shown in Route may be incomplete or out of date. The sailing instructions and official charts always take precedence.
  • Boat performance comes from polars, which may not match your boat, sails, crew or conditions.
  • AIS targets, positions shared with family and friends, and fleet comparisons depend on third-party feeds and your device. They can be delayed, wrong or missing and must not be used for collision avoidance.
  • Route needs a working device, battery and, for fresh forecasts, a data connection. It may stop working at any time.
