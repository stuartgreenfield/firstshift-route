Route

Your race, routed on every forecast

Free race routing for FirstShift subscribers, built to the RORC rules on routing aids: public forecasts, computer-generated, the same for every competitor. Choose the area your race sails in:

  • English Channel: Myth of Malham, De Guingand Bowl, Morgan Cup, Cowes Dinard St Malo, Channel Race, Cherbourg Race and your own courses in the Channel
  • Rolex Fastnet Race
  • Southern North Sea: North Sea Race and your own courses there
  • Irish Sea: St George's Channel to the North Channel, your own courses there
  • Cervantes Trophy: Cowes to Le Havre
  • Bay of Biscay: Ushant to Cape Finisterre, your own courses there
  • Celtic Sea and Western Approaches: Ushant and Brittany to the south coast of Ireland, out past Fastnet Rock, your own courses there

Mark

Inshore racing in the Solent

Round the cans or windward-leeward. Choose the course from the Solent racing marks, tap or ping the start line, and Mark shows the favoured end, the countdown, time to the line and time to burn, then the bearing and distance to the next mark.
