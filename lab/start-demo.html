DEMO

A boat (yellow) does a timed run: away on port, back on starboard, luffing to burn time, then sails for the line. Tide 1.1 kn sets towards the line here, so watch it push the boat up while it waits. The burn line is where you would need to be to hit the line at the gun at close-hauled speed with this tide. Try AP: the countdown stops, and after AP comes down the warning signal is 1 minute later and a new 5 minute sequence starts. With Z, U or the black flag, the triangle to the first mark shows in the last minute.
